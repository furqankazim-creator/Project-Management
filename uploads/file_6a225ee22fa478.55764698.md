## CONTEXT: THE WHITEBOARD MODEL

The senior consultant defined four billing types that classify every project commercially:

| Type | Code | Meaning | Invoice? | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Billed | `BILLED` | Full-rate client engagement | Yes — milestone or T&M | Moneygrid (P-3, 96%), CRM + Website |
| POC / Beta | `POC` | Proof of concept — discounted or trial rate | Partial / agreed fee | Lal Qila (LB) |
| Free of Charge | `FOC` | Goodwill, relationship building, charity | No — cost tracked only | (pipeline nurture) |
| Internal | `INTERNAL` | Our own Q1 / to-do / R&D work | No — overhead allocation | Q1 goals, Work in Mind |

These types are not static — a project can **convert** (FOC → POC → Billed) as the client relationship matures. The system must support this one-click conversion with full audit trail.

- BILLED: "Full-rate engagement. Invoices will be generated per your billing schedule. Time logs feed directly into invoice calculation."

- FOC: "No invoice will be raised. Staff hours and costs are still tracked for P&L reporting. Goodwill value is recorded for management accounts."
- INTERNAL: "Overhead project. Hours allocated to selected cost centre. No client billing. Feeds into internal capacity and cost reports."

## UPDATED: PROJECT CREATION FORM — BILLING TYPE FIELD IMPACT

When a user selects a Billing Type, the following fields across the form change:

| Billing Type | Budget Section | Services Section | Milestone Behaviour | Invoice Generated? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Billed | Show rate card + payment terms | Services have agreed rates | Each milestone can trigger invoice | Yes |
| POC/Beta | Show standard rates + discount % | Services at trial/reduced scope | Milestone triggers partial invoice | Yes (partial) |
| FOC | Show imputed cost only | Services shown for tracking | Milestones tracked, no invoice | No |
| Internal | Show internal cost centre | Services = internal only | Milestones are delivery checkpoints | No |
